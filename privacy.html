Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

What the app accesses

Personal Assistant requests read-only access to your Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly) so it can show your calendars and events inside the app.

Where your data goes

Calendar data is stored only on your own computer, in the app's local database. The OAuth tokens are stored in your operating system's keychain. The developer runs no server, and your data is never sent to the developer or sold.

If you use the app's AI assistant, calendar events relevant to your request may be included in the request the app sends to the AI provider you configured with your own API key. That provider's own privacy terms apply to those requests.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can disconnect the calendar in the app's settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all stored data.
